Self storage units in Basildon

Welcome to Shurgard Basildon, your reliable source for self-storage solutions in the heart of Basildon.

Whether you're relocating, decluttering your home, or need space for seasonal items, we provide a variety of storage units to meet your needs with convenient access.

Your local storage solution in Basildon

Shurgard Basildon is strategically located off major routes, making it easily accessible for residents in nearby areas like Lee Chapel North, Fryerning and Langdon Hills.

We cater to young professionals and families who are expanding or undergoing home renovations, offering various unit sizes suitable for everything from holiday decorations to furniture during moves.

Businesses in the area, including those near the Basildon Trade Centre, can also benefit from our practical storage solutions for inventory and equipment.

Our units are designed to keep your belongings safe and secure while providing you with the flexibility you need.

Conveniently located near key landmarks and transit hubs

Located near essential landmarks such as Basildon town centre and Wat Tyler Country Park, Shurgard Basildon is well-positioned to serve your storage needs.

Our facility is just a short drive from Eastgate Shopping Centre, making it convenient for local residents.

Public transport is readily available, with Basildon Train Station and the local bus network providing connections to surrounding areas like Billericay and Wickford.

Our covered loading dock and direct access storage units make loading and unloading simple and convenient.

Questions & answers
Is a security deposit required when renting a storage unit at Shurgard Basildon?
No, at Shurgard Basildon, you won't need to pay a security deposit when you rent your storage unit.
How easy is it to reach Shurgard Basildon if I'm coming from Lee Chapel North?
Shurgard Basildon is conveniently located just 10 minutes away from Lee Chapel North, making it easily accessible. You can drive or use local bus routes to reach us without difficulty.
What benefits does Shurgard Basildon offer compared to traditional garage storage?
Shurgard Basildon offers personal access via our app, high security with 24/7 CCTV, and flexible rental periods without the need for a long-term commitment, making it a practical alternative to traditional garage storage.
What is the minimum rental period for storage at Shurgard Basildon?
At Shurgard Basildon, the minimum rental period is just one week. Simply tell our team your move-out date when you complete your contract.
Can I buy moving boxes or packing supplies at Shurgard?
Yes, we sell quality moving boxes and tape at any Shurgard reception office.
Do I have to pay when I reserve a storage unit online at Shurgard Basildon?
No, at Shurgard Basildon, you only start your payment when you sign your rental contract.
What's the typical price for a storage unit at Shurgard Basildon?
The prices for storage units at Shurgard Basildon are designed to be affordable. Scroll up to view our available units and prices.
What security measures are in place for my storage unit at Shurgard Basildon?
Shurgard Basildon provides robust security with digital CCTV cameras, alarms monitored by a security team, sensor lighting, and perimeter fencing. Access to your unit is controlled through our app, ensuring that only you can reach your belongings.
How can I find out what size storage unit I need at Shurgard Basildon?
To determine the right size for your storage unit at Shurgard Basildon, use our online storage finder. Alternatively, you can contact our storage experts who are available to assist you with sizing.
What features does Shurgard Basildon offer for short-term business storage?
Shurgard Basildon provides facilities ideal for short-term business storage, including drive-up access and ample loading areas, making it easier for local businesses to manage inventory and tools.
Does Shurgard Basildon have facilities for large items like recreational gear?
Yes, Shurgard Basildon has spacious storage units that are perfect for larger items such as recreational gear. The units are ventilated and offer easy access for loading and unloading.
I’m renovating my home in Burstead, do you offer short-term storage solutions?
Yes, Shurgard Basildon offers flexible short-term storage solutions suitable for your renovation needs, allowing you to store your belongings securely while you work on your home.
I'm a student at South Essex College near Luckyn Lane, is Shurgard Basildon a good option for summer storage?
Yes, Shurgard Basildon is a convenient option for students moving out during the summer. Our facility is close to South Essex College, making it easy to store your belongings during your break.
Who has access to my storage unit at Shurgard Basildon?
Your storage unit at Shurgard Basildon is accessible only by you. If needed, you can authorize another person to access your unit through the Shurgard app.
