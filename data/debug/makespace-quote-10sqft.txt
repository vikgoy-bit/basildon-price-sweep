FREE move in van

Need transport to move your goods into storage? Use one of our vans for FREE! The move in vans are kept on-site and are available on a first come first serve basis, so it's best to book up early. To reserve a van for your chosen move in date, please give us a call and we'll get you booked in.

For more information, including driver criteria, you can browse our van hire page HERE

Padlock

To make sure that your unit is secure, and that only you hold the key, every storage unit must be locked with a secure padlock. We can provide the correct size and type on your move in day.

Protecting your goods

Our customers must have adequate protection for their goods whilst in storage, which we can offer under StoreProtect. Please let us know the full replacement value of the goods being stored and we'll be able to provide you with a StoreProtect quote.

ID Requirements

Before we can open a storage contract, we'll need to see a photo ID such as a Passport or Driving License, along with proof of address (e.g. a utility bill or bank statement) dated within the last 3 months. If you are unsure whether your ID is suitable, please give us a call.
